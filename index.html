You can also import a subtitle file or attach your own audio from the episode's More menu.

      Processing & playback

      Listening twice catches the occasional section whose line times come out badly wrong, and roughly doubles what transcription uses (about $0.50 instead of $0.25 per hour of video at paid prices).

      Line times are estimates, so when you click, loop or shadow a line it starts a moment before its marked time and plays a moment past its end. Automatic uses 0.6 to 0.8 s for videos Gemini transcribed (its times are only good to about a second) and 0.25 s otherwise.
